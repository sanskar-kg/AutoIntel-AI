Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. This policy explains how AutoIntel AI, operated by Kalagato, handles information when you use the AutoIntel mobile application and related services.

On this pageScopeInformation we collectHow we use itSharingSecurityYour choicesContact

1. Scope and acceptance

This Privacy Policy applies to AutoIntel AI, the AutoIntel Android application, this website, and related support interactions. By using the service, you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

AutoIntel is intended for adults and businesses using vehicle information for legitimate purposes. It is not designed to create a public directory of people or to facilitate surveillance.

2. Information we collect

Account and authentication information

Depending on the sign-in method available to you, we may receive your name, phone number, Truecaller subject or authentication identifier, Firebase authentication identifier, and account status. We use this information to create and secure your account, prevent duplicate accounts, and provide support.

Vehicle lookup information

We collect the registration number you submit, the lookup type you choose, the result returned by our service providers, timestamps, masked registration numbers for history, and the status of the request. Vehicle registrations and lookup results may be sensitive when combined with other information.

Wallet and payment information

We store wallet balance, transaction ledger entries, recharge orders, payment status, gateway identifiers, amounts, and timestamps. Payment card, bank, UPI, and other payment credentials are handled by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored by AutoIntel.

Device and technical information

We may receive app version, operating system, device model, crash reports, diagnostics, performance information, and Firebase-generated identifiers. We use this information to keep the app reliable, secure, and compatible.

Support information

If you contact us, we receive the contents of your message and any information you choose to include, such as account identifiers, screenshots, transaction references, or device details.

We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, photos, or advertising profiles for the core lookup service.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your account session.
  • Process vehicle-detail and owner-information lookups that you request.
  • Display wallet balances, recharge history, lookup history, and receipts.
  • Process payments through authorised payment providers and reconcile successful or failed transactions.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, duplicate requests, unauthorised access, and attempts to misuse sensitive information.
  • Provide support, respond to complaints, and resolve payment or lookup issues.
  • Monitor crashes, diagnose defects, improve performance, and release safer updates.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, accounting, dispute-resolution, and security obligations.

We do not use your lookup history to sell advertising profiles. We do not use personal information to make decisions about your eligibility for credit, employment, housing, insurance, or other high-impact services.

4. When we share information

We may share the minimum information needed with:

  • Lookup and infrastructure providers so a requested search can be fulfilled, stored, or secured.
  • Payment providers to create payment orders, confirm status, and prevent duplicate or fraudulent credits.
  • Firebase, hosting, analytics, and crash-reporting providers to operate the app and diagnose failures.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, or insurers where necessary and subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, legal process, or an urgent safety or fraud investigation.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose owner phone information to other users beyond the result returned through an authorised lookup, and we may withhold, mask, or restrict a result where required by law, provider rules, or our safety policies.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account, wallet, payment, and lookup records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, prevent fraud, maintain audit trails, resolve disputes, and comply with legal or accounting obligations. Crash and diagnostics data may be retained according to the settings of our service providers.

You can submit a deletion request through our account deletion form. We may need to verify your request. Deletion may be limited where records must be retained for legal, security, payment, fraud-prevention, or dispute purposes. Deleting an account does not automatically reverse a completed lookup or payment.

6. Security

We use access controls, authenticated server requests, encrypted transport, provider security controls, and limited operational access to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your device, authentication session, and any credentials or verification steps used to access the app.

If you suspect unauthorised access, contact us immediately at contactus@kalagato.co and include the relevant account or transaction reference without sending passwords, payment credentials, or authentication codes.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct, delete, or explain our handling of your personal information. You may also request restriction of processing or object to certain uses. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request.

You can stop using the app, sign out, uninstall it, or disable optional device permissions. Some information is necessary to provide authentication, wallet, payment, and lookup functionality; disabling or withholding it may prevent those features from working.

8. Children

AutoIntel is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, law, or data practices change. We will post the revised version here and update the “Last updated” date. Your continued use after an update means the revised policy applies prospectively.

10. Contact us

Questions, requests, and privacy concerns can be sent to contactus@kalagato.co. Please do not email passwords, one-time codes, card numbers, bank details, or full unmasked identity documents.

This policy is a product-facing draft for AutoIntel AI and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before a public launch or use in a regulated context.
